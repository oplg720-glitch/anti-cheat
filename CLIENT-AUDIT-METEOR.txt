IMPORTANT SERVER LIMITATION
Paper cannot query a Fabric client's installed module list through ordinary vanilla-compatible login.
The anti-cheat therefore does not claim to know whether a named module is enabled. It evaluates server-visible packets/events and player behavior.
Render-only/client UI modules such as ESP, Xray overlay, WallHack, StorageESP, Freecam camera rendering, Fullbright, Zoom, Chams, NoRender, and FreeLook cannot be reliably detected from ordinary Paper events.
Some modules that affect interaction may only be detectable when they cause impossible reach, movement, placement, attack, or packet patterns.

BEHAVIOR COVERAGE ADDED/RETAINED IN AEGISAC 1.3.3
Combat: Reach, attack-rate/AutoClicker, NoSwing, suspicious off-angle KillAura patterns, rapid multi-target attacks, explosive-item burst patterns (Crystal/Anchor/Bed aura).
Movement: Speed, Fly/Hover, Timer, ImpossibleMove, Scaffold, NoSlow heuristic, Jesus/WaterWalk heuristic, Spider heuristic, extreme Elytra movement.
World/building: FastBreak, FastPlace, Baritone mining/building/farming patterns, large schematic build pattern, Nuker/PacketMine burst pattern, AirPlace/Freecam interaction distance.
Player automation: AutoFish timing regularity, inventory/offhand automation bursts, BowSpam, chat/interaction patterns where server events expose them.
Xray: statistical ore-mining ratio heuristic only; this is not proof and can produce false positives.

NOT RELIABLY DETECTABLE BY PAPER EVENT-ONLY CHECKS
ESP, Xray visual overlay, WallHack, StorageESP, Fullbright, Chams, Freecam display-only use, CameraTweaks, Zoom, NoRender, NameProtect, BetterTab, and client-side cosmetics.
AutoTotem/AutoArmor/AutoWeapon/AutoEat/AutoGap/AntiKnockback/NoFall and some Criticals modes are only partially observable or overlap with legitimate behavior; AegisAC uses conservative suspicion signals rather than pretending to identify all variants.

No client module is identified by name or fingerprint. Only observable behavior is assessed. Low-confidence Meteor automation heuristics do not auto-ban by default.
